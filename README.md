### No. 1: [Orthopedic Slides Footwear Product Commercial](./cases/orthopedic-slides-footwear-product-commercial.md)

#### 📖 Description

A curated AI fashion prompt for Shoes · Cinematic footwear product advertisement with design and branding fidelity · Video.

#### 📝 Prompt

```
Create with seedance 2.5

Prompt 

Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 photorealistic cinematic product advertisement for a pair of olive-green and black orthopedic-style slides with a textured mesh strap and visible “DR.CARDIN” branding.

Scene 1 (0–3s): Close-up beauty shot of the slide resting on a clean white marble countertop. Soft natural daylight, elegant bright indoor setting, shallow depth of field. Tiny water droplets are visible on the olive-green strap, highlighting the water-resistant texture. Slow cinematic camera push-in.

Scene 2 (3–6s): A hand picks up the slide and gently tilts and rotates it, showing the thick cushioned sole, textured footbed, breathable mesh strap, and overall construction. Smooth controlled product-camera movement, realistic hand interaction, premium commercial lighting.

Scene 3 (6–8s): Extreme close-up of the textured olive-green strap and black cushioned sole, emphasizing material quality, stitching, ventilation and ergonomic design. Slow lateral camera movement with strong macro detail.

Scene 4 (8–10s): A person wearing the slide steps naturally onto a clean light-colored tiled floor. Show the sandal from a low-angle perspective as the foot slides comfortably inside. End with a clean product-focused shot and soft background blur.

Style: ultra-realistic commercial footwear advertisement, premium product photography, natural soft lighting, realistic textures, cinematic depth of field, smooth camera motion, subtle film look, clean minimalist home interior, highly detailed, realistic proportions, no distortion, no extra objects, preserve the exact sandal design and branding.
```

Prompt 👇
Create an exactly 30-second vertical fashion film, Vogue editorial × Tokyo street fashion × cinematic luxury, with fast energetic pacing and sharp cuts.

### No. 3: [Retro Running Sneaker Ad Storyboard](./cases/retro-running-sneaker-ad-storyboard.md)

#### 📖 Description

A curated AI fashion prompt for Shoes · Multi-panel sports sneaker advertising storyboard with hero product shots, macro footwear detail, and cross-panel shoe consistency · Video.

#### 📝 Prompt

```
Create with seedance 2.5

Prompt 

Master Prompt

Create a premium cinematic 6-panel sports sneaker advertising storyboard / campaign contact sheet in a vertical 2×3 layout. Use the uploaded image as the visual reference for the overall composition, camera language, lighting, styling, and product presentation.

Product: red-and-white retro running sneakers with visible air-cushion soles, textured red upper, white mesh panels, white laces, and a small swoosh-style logo. Make the sneaker the hero product throughout.
Model: young adult male, slim/athletic build, short slightly curly dark hair, black rectangular glasses, subtle facial hair, wearing a black lightweight Nike-style jacket, plain white T-shirt, loose black cargo pants, black backpack, and the same red-and-white sneakers. Maintain the same model identity, outfit, hairstyle, and footwear across every panel.

Panel 01 — OPENING / HERO SHOT

Cinematic low-angle full-body shot of the male model walking confidently through a modern downtown city street at golden hour. Tall glass skyscrapers surrounding him, warm sunrise/sunset light, dramatic perspective, shallow depth of field, realistic reflections on wet pavement. The red sneakers prominently visible in the foreground. Premium sports campaign photography.

Panel 02 — ACTION / RUNNING

Dynamic low-angle tracking shot of the same model sprinting through the city. One foot dramatically extended toward the camera, red-and-white sneaker clearly visible. Strong motion blur in the background, moving cars, skyscrapers, realistic body movement, energetic athletic composition, cinematic sunlight.

Panel 03 — DETAIL / MACRO

Extreme macro product photography of the red-and-white sneaker. Focus on stitching, fabric texture, laces, logo, mesh, leather details and air-cushion sole. Ultra-sharp material details, realistic shadows, premium commercial lighting, shallow depth of field, luxury sneaker advertisement aesthetic.

Panel 04 — CLIMBING / URBAN ADVENTURE

Low-angle shot of the same model climbing concrete urban stairs. Camera positioned close to the ground behind/below him, emphasizing the sneaker sole and grip. One foot stepping upward, visible tread pattern and cushioning. Modern glass buildings in the background, dramatic perspective, natural daylight, dynamic athletic pose.

Panel 05 — AERIAL / DRONE VIEW

High-angle drone shot looking directly down at the same model standing on a rooftop or elevated urban walkway. City streets, cars and skyscrapers visible far below. The model looks upward toward the camera. Red sneakers clearly visible against the dark outfit and geometric city environment. Cinematic urban composition, realistic depth and scale.

Panel 06 — CLOSING / LIFESTYLE PRODUCT HERO

Premium product-only hero shot of the same red-and-white sneakers placed on a concrete rooftop ledge overlooking a modern city skyline at sunset. Warm golden sunlight, beautiful orange sky, subtle lens flare, soft bokeh skyscrapers, dramatic shadows, luxurious commercial sneaker photography.

Overall style: ultra-realistic commercial photography, cinematic sports campaign, premium Nike-style advertising aesthetic, dramatic low angles, realistic human anatomy, natural skin texture, physically accurate materials, crisp sneaker details, golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, high dynamic range, realistic motion blur, professional color grading, sophisticated composition, photorealistic, 8K detail.

Layout: six equal panels arranged 2 columns × 3 rows, clean editorial storyboard/contact-sheet presentation, consistent model and sneaker identity across all six scenes, each panel visually distinct but part of the same advertising campaign.

Negative prompt: distorted anatomy, extra fingers, duplicate limbs, inconsistent face, different model between panels, different shoes, warped sneaker logos, malformed soles, floating objects, plastic skin, cartoon style, low resolution, excessive blur, oversaturated colors, incorrect perspective, duplicated subjects, messy composition, unreadable typography.
```
