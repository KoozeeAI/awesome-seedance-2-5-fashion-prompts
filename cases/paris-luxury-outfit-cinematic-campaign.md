# Paris Luxury Outfit Cinematic Campaign

## Prompt

What if you could turn ONE fashion reference image into a cinematic Paris luxury campaign?

I’m testing this workflow with Wizstar + Seedance 2.5.

One character.
One outfit.
One continuous walk.
Cinematic camera movement.
Paris + Eiffel Tower.
Luxury fashion-film aesthetic.

The key is giving Seedance 2.5 a detailed prompt for character consistency, camera movement, transitions, lighting and timing.

Try creating your own cinematic fashion film with @WizstarAI and Seedance 2.5👇

Prompt -

15-second, 16:9 horizontal luxury fashion film set on a romantic Paris street. Photorealistic cinematic 35mm photography, premium, elegant, vibrant, rhythmic, high-fashion editorial aesthetic.

Use Image 1 as the ONLY main character. Keep her face, blonde hair, body proportions, red luxury outfit, black pointed heels and handbag perfectly consistent throughout. No face changes, outfit changes, duplicates, disappearances or teleportation. She continuously walks forward along the same Paris street with natural, confident, effortless movement.

0–4s — HERO ENTRY:
Start with an ultra-low camera gliding quickly over Paris cobblestones at shoe level, then smoothly rise as the woman walks toward camera. Reveal her full red outfit against elegant cream Parisian architecture, balconies, cafés and the Eiffel Tower in the distance. Golden sunlight creates a cinematic hero entrance.

4–8s — DYNAMIC FOLLOW:
Camera smoothly moves from frontal tracking to a side angle, then circles toward a 45-degree rear angle while she keeps walking naturally. Her blonde hair moves gently in the breeze. Red outfit contrasts beautifully with the cream architecture and warm sunlight.

8–11s — FASHION CLOSE-UP:
Use a natural architectural column as a seamless foreground transition. Camera moves closer into a side-front medium shot, showing her face, upper body, handbag and walking motion. Capture realistic fabric texture, buttons, jewelry, flowing hair and natural hand movement. Do not use extreme macro shots.

11–13s — HEELS:
Camera continuously tilts downward into a low-angle tracking shot of her black pointed heels stepping confidently across the cobblestones. Correct anatomy, consistent shoes and realistic footsteps. The camera then smoothly begins rising again.

13–15s — EPIC ENDING:
Camera rapidly but smoothly pulls back from her face to a full-body and wide Paris city view. She remains the visual focus in the center of the street. Reveal cafés, architecture, trees and the Eiffel Tower under golden sunlight. End on a premium luxury fashion magazine cover composition.

STYLE:
Luxury fashion-house commercial, realistic 35mm film texture, shallow depth of field, precise focus transitions, natural lens flare, realistic physics, cinematic lighting, smooth purposeful camera movement. No shaky camera, cheap music-video look, excessive slow motion, robotic posing or runway walking.

MUSIC:
Modern high-fashion electronic music with a sophisticated, powerful rhythm. Build tension from 0–8s, synchronize subtle beats with footsteps from 8–13s, then reach a cinematic climax during the final pullback.
