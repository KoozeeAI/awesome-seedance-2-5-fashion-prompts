# Champagne Silk Dress Cinematic Campaign

A curated AI fashion prompt for Dress · Multi-shot luxury fashion film with dress consistency and fabric movement · Video.

## Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic luxury fashion film featuring an elegant young woman in a flowing champagne-gold silk dress, carrying a beautiful bouquet of vibrant orange tulips mixed with delicate white flowers. Her hair is styled in a loose, effortless bun, with soft strands framing her face. She walks through a picturesque European street filled with charming cafés, vintage architecture, and warm late-afternoon sunlight.

SHOT 01 — Cinematic Entrance: Low-angle tracking shot following her elegant heels across textured cobblestones. The camera smoothly tilts upward, revealing her flowing dress and colorful bouquet as sunlight streams between historic buildings.

SHOT 02 — Dynamic Side Tracking: The camera glides beside her as she walks past outdoor cafés. Her dress moves naturally in the breeze, and she glances toward the camera with a subtle, confident smile.

SHOT 03 — Floral Beauty Close-Up: She gently lifts the bouquet toward her face. The camera makes a slow 180-degree orbit around her as she smells the tulips, with individual petals glowing in the golden sunlight.

SHOT 04 — Editorial Portrait: Tight close-up of her face, natural skin texture, expressive eyes, and soft smile. A gentle breeze moves a few strands of hair while the background melts into beautiful cinematic bokeh.

SHOT 05 — Over-the-Shoulder Reveal: The camera moves behind her shoulder, revealing a beautiful European street with café terraces, pedestrians, and warm sunlight reflecting off old stone buildings. She turns her head slightly toward the camera.

SHOT 06 — Dramatic Final Shot: Smooth backward tracking shot as she walks toward the camera, holding the bouquet elegantly. The camera gradually rises into a wider composition, capturing her silhouette against the glowing sunset.

Visual style: High-end luxury perfume advertisement, European summer romance, editorial fashion photography, photorealistic details, realistic human movement, natural facial expressions, cinematic lens flares, warm amber highlights, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens aesthetics, subtle film grain, premium color grading, 4K detail.

Consistency: Keep the same face, body proportions, hairstyle, dress, jewelry, bouquet, and environment throughout all shots. Natural hand anatomy, realistic fabric physics, believable walking motion, seamless transitions, no flickering, no distorted flowers, no text, no logos, no watermark.

Format: Vertical 9:16, cinematic pacing, approximately 10–12 seconds.
